01

Services

For foreign manufacturers and for local importers and distributors in Saudi Arabia. Every engagement is aligned with the SFDA requirements and MDS guidance that apply to it.

SFDA licences and registrations

ARL

Authorized Representative Licence

Securing the licence that allows a Saudi-based authorized representative to act on behalf of a foreign manufacturer before SFDA.

MDEL

Medical Device Establishment Licence

Licensing for importers, distributors and other local establishments that handle medical devices and IVDs in the Kingdom.

MDMA

Medical Device Marketing Authorization

New registrations, changes and renewals for Class A to D medical devices and IVDs, prepared and submitted end to end.

MDIL

Medical Device Import Licence

Import licensing so registered products can be cleared and shipped into Saudi Arabia without delays at the border.

PCS

Product Classification Service

Applying to SFDA to confirm whether a product is regulated as a medical device, IVD, drug or other category before investing in registration.

NCMDR

NCMDR queries

Handling queries from SFDA's National Center for Medical Devices Reporting, including adverse event reports, field safety notices and recall follow-ups.

UDI

UDI listing

Registering your devices' Unique Device Identification data on the SFDA Saudi-DI database, so products meet labeling and traceability requirements.

Regulatory support

MDS-REQ1

Technical file review

Section-by-section review of your technical file for alignment with MDS-REQ1, before it reaches the SFDA reviewer.

MDS-G028

Strategic bundling plan

Grouping product families, models and accessories under MDS-G028 to reduce the number of applications and fees.

MDS-G008

Classification guidance

Determining the correct risk class under MDS-G008, with the applicable rule documented for your file.

Gap analysis

Gap analysis

A clear report of every missing or weak document, where it sits in your file, and what SFDA accepts in its place.

Queries

SFDA query support

Structured responses to SFDA reviewer comments and returned applications, so your file moves forward on the next round.

Drafting

Drafting justifications

Written justifications for equivalence, exemptions, test omissions and other positions SFDA asks you to explain.

Templates

Supporting templates

Ready-to-use declarations, letters, checklists and document templates that match SFDA expectations.

How an engagement runs

A clear sequence, with you knowing where your file stands at each step.

  1. Step 1

    Scoping call

    We confirm classification, reference markets and the fastest eligible route.

  2. Step 2

    Gap analysis

    Your existing documents are checked against SFDA requirements and past reviewer comments.

  3. Step 3

    File completion

    Missing or weak sections are drafted or corrected with your team.

  4. Step 4

    Submission

    The application is filed and tracked through SFDA review.

  5. Step 5

    Approval and after

    Deficiency responses, MDMA issue, then changes and renewals as your portfolio grows.
